INTRODUCTION

Who are we?

Our company was founded by private individuals in 1991. We carry out construction work throughout Hungary and abroad.

Our main profile is the installation of complex industrial and building services thermal insulation structures, together with the related works (corrosion protection, scaffolding, etc.). Our goal is to create thermal-bridge-free, energy-saving and environmentally friendly systems using modern materials and technologies, tailored to the client's needs. To carry out the work, our company has a team of trained technical and skilled professionals.

To increase our competitiveness, we introduced a quality assurance system in 1999, to the satisfaction of our clients.
