FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Have a question? We have summarised what people ask most about our company and services. Can't find what you're looking for? Give us a call, we're happy to help!

What does Paradis Kft. do?

Our main profile is the installation of complex industrial and building services thermal insulation structures, together with the related works, such as corrosion protection and scaffolding.

What services do you offer?

Thermal insulation, cold insulation, acoustic insulation, fire protection insulation, industrial sheet metal cladding, scaffolding and corrosion protection work, as well as the sale of insulation materials.

Where have you worked?

Our headquarters are in Debrecen (Balmazújvárosi út 10.), but we carry out construction work throughout Hungary and abroad.

Which sectors do you work in?

We provide building services thermal insulation in shopping centres, office buildings, hospitals and hotels, among others. We provide technological thermal insulation in power plants, the oil and gas industry, chemical plants, pharmaceutical factories, the food industry, the automotive industry and cold storage facilities.

What materials do you work with?

We use materials from well-known manufacturers, such as Rockwool, Armacell, Kaiflex and Promat products.

What guarantees a quality result?

Our quality assurance system, 30+ years of experience, trained professionals and materials from well-known manufacturers. Our work is backed by domestic and international references.

What principles do you work by?

Our goal is to create thermal-bridge-free, energy-saving and environmentally friendly systems using modern materials and technologies, tailored to the client's needs.

Can I see your previous work?

Yes, you can find our domestic and international references, as well as our photo gallery, under the References menu.
